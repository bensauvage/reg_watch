EU AML Package — prospective timeline to Level 2/3 live dates

Prepared 23 July 2026 from AMLA / European Commission official sources. Best case = 3+3+3 model (AMLA review, Commission adoption, EP/Council non-objection); realistic scenario assumes a longer Commission stage. ITS have no EP/Council veto; Guidelines skip the Commission (application aligned to 10 Jul 2027). Italic = pipeline instruments, dates estimated from AMLA SPD 2026–2028. Not legal advice.

How to use the links: instrument name → the Level 1 “original text” on EUR-Lex (empowering article’s regulation/directive). Clicking a bar opens the document for that stage: blue → consultation paper/page; yellow → AMLA final draft; red → adopted act in the Official Journal. Where a stage’s text is not yet released (most of them, for now), the click falls back to the most advanced document actually available — the tooltip tells you which. These fallbacks will be replaced as texts are published.
